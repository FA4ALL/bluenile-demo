Our story

A Pune institution on Bund Garden Road

For decades, Blue Nile has been where Pune comes for biryani: students, families, office teams and anyone passing through Camp with an appetite.

The recipes have stayed true to the Irani and Mughlai style that made the name, and the doors open early, so breakfast, lunch or a late dinner are all covered.
